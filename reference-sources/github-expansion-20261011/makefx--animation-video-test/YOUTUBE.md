Recipe for this video
- Picture: 18 HTML scenes rendered to video with HyperFrames (open source), 3840×2160, 24 fps, 40 s.
- Made by: Claude (Opus 5.5) in Claude Code. Five agents wrote the scenes in parallel from one brief; every beat was checked frame by frame.
- Look: makefx's own design system: the canvas, real product cards, amber only on the thing being acted on.
- Rhythm: 120 BPM, one 2 s bar per scene, one event per eighth note.
- Sound: synthesized by a script: music that follows the chapters, and 169 sound effects placed on the on-screen events.
- Media: the harbour pictures are drawn stand-ins; prices and balances follow makefx's price sheet.
