# Period painting references actually viewed

- Canaletto, *Piazza San Marco*, late 1720s. The Metropolitan Museum of Art, object 435839. https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/435839 . Image: Canaletto-Piazza-San-Marco.jpg.
- Hubert Robert, *The Portico of a Country Mansion*, 1773. The Metropolitan Museum of Art, object 437468. https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/437468 . Image: Hubert-Robert-Portico-1773.jpg.

Both images were opened and visually inspected. Used as composition and architectural references, not as film textures. Shared principles: articulated cornices and pilasters, rhythmic but differentiated facades, warm sun/cool shadows, very small people against substantial masonry, deep arcades, stairs and foreground framing. The fictional city retains its own geography and functioning machinery.
