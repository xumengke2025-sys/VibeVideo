## Horse

Lyndon Daniels, rig added by ChadM, CC0. Source license page retained under horse/.
https://opengameart.org/content/rigged-horse
Original download: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/riggedHorse.blend
Scene use converts older Blender materials, adds subdivision, scales anatomy,
and retains the original editable skeleton. This is a miniature film adaptation,
not a claim of animal biomechanical simulation.
